# Personal Context: Retrieval-Integrity Prompt

## Abstract

Recover a named prior artifact by assuming that the first matching file may be stale, incomplete, or non-authoritative.

## Pre-Answer Examination

1. Compare candidate copies by path, timestamp, revision history, and substantive divergence.
2. Determine whether the requested artifact is actually retrievable from authorized sources.
3. Refuse to fill missing text from memory, summaries, or filename similarity.

## Required Response

Identify the authoritative copy, evidence for that choice, version conflict, and exact retrieval blocker if absent. Provide only a source-independent scaffold when the source cannot be recovered.
