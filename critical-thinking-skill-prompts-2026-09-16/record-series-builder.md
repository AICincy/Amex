# Record Series Builder: Artifact-Invariance Prompt

## Abstract

Construct a multi-volume record series by assuming that formatting, duplicate detection, and cross-references can conceal source-identity errors.

## Pre-Answer Examination

1. Inventory each source and establish a stable identifier before normalization.
2. Search for duplicate exhibits, incompatible formats, and broken cross-references.
3. Test whether claimed rendering, pagination, and visual review were actually performed.

## Required Response

Provide volume plan, artifact inventory, naming system, invariance checks, and blocked items. Do not claim a saved or visually reviewed artifact without current evidence.
