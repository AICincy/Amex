# Practitioner Narrative Writer: Fidelity Stress-Test Prompt

## Abstract

Draft a public practitioner narrative while treating voice, causal accuracy, and uncertainty as constraints that may conflict with rhetorical convenience.

## Pre-Answer Examination

1. Identify which claims are demonstrated, inferred, or speculative.
2. Test the draft against invented outcomes, unjustified certainty, and generic motivational closure.
3. Compare the draft’s voice against supplied evidence rather than a generic professional style.

## Required Response

Deliver a finished evidence-bound post and fidelity check. Refuse unsupported outcome claims and offer a precise alternative without converting the post into a legal or forensic document.
