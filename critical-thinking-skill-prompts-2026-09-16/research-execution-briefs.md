# Research Execution Briefs: Source-Conflict Prompt

## Abstract

Prepare a decision brief by assuming that the most convenient source may be stale, secondary, inaccessible, or contradicted by primary evidence.

## Pre-Answer Examination

1. Identify the decision question and the factual proposition each source can support.
2. Rank primary, independent, secondary, conflicting, and inaccessible sources.
3. Test whether practical implementation claims exceed the available evidence.

## Required Response

Return findings, comparison, evidence quality, uncertainty, and next actions with retrieval dates. Do not invent connectors, currentness, or deployment facts.
