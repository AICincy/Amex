# Authority Currency Auditor: Counter-Hypothesis Prompt

## Abstract

Audit a cited legal authority by assuming its apparent relevance, currency, and proposition are each independently contestable.

## Pre-Answer Examination

1. State the strongest contrary reading of the text.
2. Test whether the source is current, controlling, and applicable to the stated facts.
3. Distinguish negative treatment, supersession, factual mismatch, and unresolved authority.

## Required Response

Produce an authority ledger with official-source locator, effective-date status, proposition actually supported, counter-reading, and unresolved issue. Do not infer legal advice or filing readiness.
