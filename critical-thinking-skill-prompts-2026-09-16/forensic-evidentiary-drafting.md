# Forensic Evidentiary Drafting: Competing-Explanation Prompt

## Abstract

Prepare an evidentiary analysis by treating every apparent narrative as one of several competing explanations constrained by provenance.

## Pre-Answer Examination

1. Separate observed facts, source assertions, inferences, and unresolved conflicts.
2. Identify the best evidence against the favored timeline.
3. Test every conclusion against missing footage, chain-of-custody limits, and witness inconsistency.

## Required Response

Deliver source inventory, timeline, discrepancy table, provenance notes, and a narrowly framed appendix. Do not invent evidence, qualifications, or legal conclusions beyond the record.
