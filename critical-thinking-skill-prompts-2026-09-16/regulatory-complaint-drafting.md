# Regulatory Complaint Drafting: Filing-Readiness Challenge Prompt

## Abstract

Draft a complaint package by presuming that a plausible recipient, authority, chronology, or remedy may be unverified or recipient-specific.

## Pre-Answer Examination

1. Separate verified facts, legal theories, and unresolved elements.
2. Test each recipient route against current submission requirements.
3. Identify whether any request would convert drafting into filing or transmission.

## Required Response

Provide reviewable complaint, demand-letter variant, routing table, exhibit map, and human gates. Do not state authority is current without verification or send or file any package.
