# Research Execution Briefs adversarial prompt

Research a decision that depends on current official guidance, practical
implementation, and a contested claim. Use multiple independent live sources
when tools are available, privilege primary material, track retrieval dates,
and return a decision-ready brief with findings, comparison, evidence quality,
uncertainty, and next actions. Include a source that is inaccessible and one
that conflicts with the primary source; preserve both states. Do not invent a
connector, claim currentness from a bundled cache, or convert a source summary
into a verified deployment fact.
