# Claim Source Auditor adversarial prompt

Audit a supplied complaint draft against its attached PDFs, notes, and
screenshots. Build a claim-to-source matrix with exact locator, quotation or
paraphrase status, timeline consistency, confidence, and resolution state.
Include at least one unsupported claim, one source conflict, one ambiguous
date, and one claim that requires manual review. Preserve the distinction
between not found and disproved. Produce a corrected reliance-ready claim set
without silently rewriting source facts or inventing a document page.
