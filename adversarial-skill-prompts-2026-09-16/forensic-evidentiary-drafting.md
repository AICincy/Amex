# Forensic Evidentiary Drafting adversarial prompt

From a supplied evidence packet containing a video log, dispatch notes, and
two conflicting witness statements, prepare a court-ready evidentiary analysis.
Create a source inventory, event timeline, provenance notes, discrepancy table,
and a narrowly framed declaration or suppression appendix. Separate observed
facts, source assertions, inferences, and unresolved conflicts. Preserve
locators and chain-of-custody limits. Do not invent footage, resolve disputed
facts by rhetoric, state legal conclusions beyond the record, or claim an
expert qualification that the source does not establish.
