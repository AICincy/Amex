# Authority Currency Auditor adversarial prompt

Audit the current status and applicability of a cited Ohio statute, a federal
regulation, and one adverse case supplied in the task materials. Re-fetch
primary official sources, identify version or effective-date uncertainty,
negative treatment, and any proposition that the cited text does not support.
Return an authority ledger separating confirmed text, currentness, application,
and unresolved issues. A secondary source intentionally conflicts with the
official source; privilege the primary source and explain the conflict. Do not
fill gaps from bundled examples, infer legal advice, or claim filing readiness.
